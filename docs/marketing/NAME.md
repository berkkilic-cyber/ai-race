# Name shortlist

"AI Race" is the working title. It is accurate, but it is a phrase, not a name: it cannot be searched, owned or told apart from news headlines, and several games already describe themselves with it (see "The neighbourhood" below). This page proposes twelve names, checks each for games or apps with the same or a confusingly similar name, and picks three.

**What was checked, and what was not.** For each name I ran web searches for the name with "game", "Steam", "App Store" and "Google Play" (25 November 2026) and opened the store pages that looked close. Web search is not the same as searching inside the App Store and Google Play apps, which rank and filter differently, so "nothing found" below means "nothing surfaced in web search", not "free". **I could not check trademarks** (that needs the USPTO, EUIPO and TÜRKPATENT databases and ideally a lawyer), and **I could not check social handles**, because that needs logged-in accounts on each platform. The owner should search all three trademark registers and both stores, and try to register the handles, **before committing to a name**.

## Criteria

1. Says "AI lab race" or "satire" within a second.
2. Easy to say and spell for English and Turkish speakers. Turkish players know "AGI", "hype" and "speedrun" (docs/GLOSSARY.tr.md keeps AGI and Hype in English); they do not reliably know "compute", "frontier" or English idioms.
3. Not "Tycoon" (two competitors use it).
4. Distinct from AI Lab Tycoon, Model Co., AI Lab Tycoon: Misaligned, KI Lab, and from Plague Inc. (so no "Inc." and nothing about plagues or outbreaks).
5. No real company, product, model or person, and no catchphrase owned by one.

## Top three

### 1. AGI Speedrun

- **Why:** two words, both already English loanwords in Turkish gaming, and together they are the whole game: a race to AGI, played fast, with a Sprint mode that fits the word. It is a joke a gamer gets before the screenshot loads, and it is unlike every neighbour (none uses "speedrun").
- **Risks:** "speedrun" might suggest a platformer to a few people; the store subtitle fixes that ("Run an AI lab. Win the world."). AI researchers use "speedrun" for benchmark tasks, which helps more than it hurts.
- **Store check:** nothing with the name found (links in the table).
- **Handles to try:** @agispeedrun on X, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Reddit (r/AGISpeedrun), Discord server name "AGI Speedrun". Fallbacks: @agispeedrungame, @playagispeedrun.

### 2. Safety Third

- **Why:** the funniest of the set and the one that carries the satire: a lab that ranks safety third is the game's whole tone. Short, easy to say in both languages.
- **Risks:** it does not say "AI" on its own; it needs a subtitle everywhere ("Safety Third: an AI lab race"), and Turkish players may miss the pun. A small itch.io game uses "safety 3rd", and the phrase is a common joke used elsewhere: both need a trademark check.
- **Handles to try:** @safetythirdgame (plain @safetythird is likely gone: it is a common phrase), r/SafetyThirdGame.

### 3. AGI Rush

- **Why:** the plainest and most pronounceable option, very short, says exactly what the game is in both languages.
- **Risks:** closest to an existing competitor's subtitle ("Race to AGI"); generic enough that store search will be crowded. Choose it only if the first two fail checks.
- **Handles to try:** @agirush, @agirushgame.

## What the owner must do before choosing

1. Search the App Store and Google Play apps (Türkiye and US storefronts) for each finalist and its close variants.
2. Search trademarks for each finalist in classes 9 and 41 (games and entertainment): USPTO, EUIPO and TÜRKPATENT. If unsure, a short paid check by a trademark attorney is cheaper than renaming after launch.
3. Try to register the handles above on X, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit and Discord, and check the .com domain. Pick the name whose handles you can actually get.
4. Then update `app.json` (`expo.name`), the store texts in STORE.md and the strings that print the name (the start screen title comes from the pack).
